How it works

Two small contracts and one signature from the lender.

1

The wallet gets Errand's code

The lender signs one EIP-7702 authorization. Their address, key and balance stay the same; the wallet can now take calls from Reactive's callback proxy, and only from their own Errand.

2

They ask to exit, once

The wallet calls requestExit() on itself. ErrandMorpho on Reactive Network sees it and starts watching this market's repays and deposits.

3

Every freed coin goes home

On each repay or deposit, ErrandMorpho sends a callback. The wallet withdraws whatever is free, and Errand stands down when nothing is left in the market.

Why not a bot?

A bot that withdraws for you needs your key or your funds in its vault. Here the event itself is the trigger and the withdrawal comes from the lender's own address. Reactive's callback proxy tells the wallet which contract sent the request, so the wallet obeys only its own Errand.
